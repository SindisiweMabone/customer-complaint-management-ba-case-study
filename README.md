# Customer Complaint Management System

## Business Analysis Case Study

### Project Overview

This fictional case study demonstrates how business analysis techniques can be used to investigate and improve a customer complaint management process. The project follows the journey from identifying a business problem and understanding the current process to defining requirements, documenting user stories and acceptance criteria, and validating the proposed solution.

The purpose of this project is to demonstrate practical skills in business analysis, requirements analysis, process improvement, systems thinking, and solution validation.

> **Note:** This is a fictional portfolio project created for professional development and does not contain confidential information from any organisation or client.

---

## Business Problem

The organisation currently manages customer complaints through multiple channels, including email, telephone calls and manual records. This makes it difficult to consistently capture, track and manage complaints throughout their lifecycle.

As a result, the organisation faces challenges such as:

* Complaints being recorded inconsistently.
* Limited visibility of complaint status and ownership.
* Difficulty tracking response and resolution times.
* Risk of complaints being overlooked or duplicated.
* Limited reporting on recurring complaint types and trends.

The organisation requires a centralised system to improve the capture, tracking, management and reporting of customer complaints.

---

## Project Objectives

The proposed solution aims to:

1. Provide a centralised platform for recording and managing customer complaints.
2. Improve visibility of complaint status, ownership and progress.
3. Support the assignment and tracking of complaints throughout their lifecycle.
4. Reduce the risk of complaints being lost, duplicated or overlooked.
5. Improve the ability to identify complaint trends and recurring issues.
6. Support more effective reporting and decision-making.

---

## Scope

This case study focuses on analysing the requirements for a customer complaint management system.

### In Scope

* Complaint capture and registration.
* Complaint categorisation.
* Complaint assignment and ownership.
* Complaint status tracking.
* Complaint investigation and resolution recording.
* Notifications and updates.
* Complaint reporting and trend analysis.

### Out of Scope

* Development of the actual software application.
* Integration with external systems.
* Customer payment or compensation processing.
* Detailed technical architecture and infrastructure design.

---

## Analysis Approach

The following business analysis activities are covered in this project:

1. Define and analyse the business problem.
2. Identify key stakeholders and their needs.
3. Document the current complaint management process.
4. Identify process gaps and improvement opportunities.
5. Define business and functional requirements.
6. Create user stories and acceptance criteria.
7. Document the proposed process improvements.
8. Define test scenarios to validate the solution against requirements.
