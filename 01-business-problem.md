# Business Problem Analysis

## 1. Current Situation

Customers use telephone calls and email to log complaints with the business. Some customers contact customer service, while others send complaints directly to departments. When a complaint is received, the initial recipient will either attempt to resolve it or forward it to the relevant department. As complaints move between employees or departments, ownership can become unclear.

Complaint information is not consistently updated in the shared Excel spreadsheet. Employees may assume another person will update the record after a complaint is forwarded, forget to update it, or, in the case of newer employees, may not be aware that the spreadsheet exists. This results in incomplete complaint records.

When customers follow up, the employee handling the enquiry may need to ask other employees or departments to establish the current status of the complaint, as the information may not be immediately visible. Complaints are generally expected to be resolved within five working days, but this timeframe is not formally tracked.

## 2. Core Business Problem

The organisation lacks reliable visibility of customer complaints throughout their lifecycle. This limits the organisation's ability to understand complaint volumes, monitor progress and resolution, identify recurring issues, and make informed improvements to the customer experience.
